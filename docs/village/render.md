# Village: paths, villagers, terrain marks and pads

The roads the sim lays, how people and worn ground are drawn, and how buildings are seated on the terrain. The index is the Village subsystem table in `AGENTS.md`.

## Paths

`sim/pathNetwork.ts` (nodes and painting in `pathGraph.ts`, searched field tracks in `pathTracks.ts`): **ring roads** follow the rings (`ringModel.roadRadiusAt`, midway between two rings of houses); ring 0 is the plaza loop, laid at founding. The main road runs from the temple door along its axis and grows ring by ring (`growAxisRoad` in `pathNetwork.ts`), ending just past the outermost ring with houses. A new building adds a node on its ring's road (joined to the angular neighbours along the road, `ARC_MAX_M`), a short stub from its doorstep, and, if that road is not yet connected to the temple, **radial lanes** inward ring by ring (extras with `RADIAL_P`); lanes slide along the ring to slip between houses. The planner keeps houses between their ring's road and the next (`planner.clearOfRoads`) and off laid lanes. Only field tracks and rare fallbacks are searched by A* (merging into existing paths, `AFFINITY.TRACK`). Everything is painted into the wear field with a permanent floor; foot traffic deepens it. Villagers mostly follow paths: per-journey affinity (70% followers, 30% cutters) over a pathiness grid, 60% of strolls aimed at paths (`PATHS.AFFINITY`, `WANDER_ON_PATH_SHARE`)

## Villagers

`render/villagerRenderer.ts` — one instanced draw per figure, role × sex (12 chunky Norse figures from `public/models/village-kit/villagers.glb`, 830 to 1,200 triangles each, built by `assets-src/village/villager_build.py` and converted by `assets/villagerKit.ts`; a figure missing from the GLB falls back to its placeholder in `assets/placeholder/villagerFigure.ts`; plain `Mesh` + `InstancedBufferGeometry`: `InstancedMesh` applies instancing *before* `positionNode`), all sharing the GPU rig in `villagerMaterial.ts` (rigid parts swung about their joints in the vertex shader; poses idle, walk, hammer, pray, carry, chop, hoe, fish; the tool shows only while working, the priest's staff except while praying; colour alpha = slot / 3 (`VillagerSlot`): skin, hair and tunic are tinted per villager from `variant` 0..255); soft contact shadows in `blobShadows.ts` (they do not cast into shadow maps). Roles: builder, priest, farmer, woodcutter, fisherman, idler; woodcutters and fishermen are ambient (no resource yet) and only exist when the map has trees or a reachable shore near the village (`VILLAGERS.WOODCUTTER_SHARE`, `FISHERMAN_SHARE`). Console: `__pantheonVillage.lineup()` stands every figure in a row, `people(role)` counts and locates them. The rig pivots (`VILLAGER_RIG`, mirrored in `villager_build.py`), part ids and slots are the interface between the Blender build and the game.

Crew members at a remote site are ordinary pool villagers far from the village; they draw and cast blob shadows like the rest within the villager draw distance. The site and camp are placeholder props ([remote-sites](remote-sites.md)).

## Terrain marks

`render/villageTerrainLayers.ts` — grass exclusion map (`grassSystem.propExclusionMap`), prop AO and the path channel (R) of the terrain overlay map, composed from a base snapshot + stamps + wear. The wear field reports changed **tiles** (`WEAR.TILE_CELLS`), and the layers compose and upload them per tile under a per-frame budget (`VISUAL.village.TERRAIN`); decay only dirties tiles whose visible value changes. Worn ground has **no hard edges**: the layers read `wear.sampleFeathered` (a cell keeps its value across the cell and fades over one cell beyond it, so one-cell paths keep their width) for the crisp core and `wear.sampleHalo` (a `WEAR.HALO_CELLS` tent blur, refreshed per dirty tile in `takeDirty`) for the skirt: dirt up to `PATH.HALO_MAX`, grass thinned by up to `GRASS.HALO_STRENGTH` (kept above the blade cull, so the skirt only shortens blades). Stamps use wide fades (`YARD.FADE_M`, `GRASS_FADE_M`). DEV terrain-AO re-bakes re-apply the village shade through `onPropContactAoRebaked`. No new terrain texture (terrain is at the 16-sampler budget)

## Pads and the detail layer

The sim plans on the original ground (`createTerrainSampler` uses `sampleChiseledWorldYBase`) and seats every building on a level pad (`sim/pad.ts`, `planPad`): the floor `plinthTopY` is the mean ground over the 2 m detail nodes of every cell the footprint touches, `plinthBaseY` the lowest. There is no plinth mesh: `render/terraformView.ts` + `render/terraformer.ts` level the terrain under each pad into the **2 m detail layer** (`terrain/cpu/fineDelta.ts`: offsets on a 4x finer lattice over a square of `VILLAGE.PLINTH.REGION_HALF_M` around the seed, border zero, original grids untouched). Pad nodes land exactly on the floor, a `SKIRT_M` skirt fades back, overlapping claims average by build progress. Progress drives it: fraction = foundation-phase `progress / WORK_S[0]`, 1 from stage 1 on; founded buildings are settled before the first draw. Each flush (4 Hz) rebakes the patch mesh, re-seats static map props (`resnapMapProps`) and dressing (`dressing.resnap`), invalidates grass blade heights (`GrassSystem.invalidateTerrainHeights`) and marks the shadow maps dirty.
